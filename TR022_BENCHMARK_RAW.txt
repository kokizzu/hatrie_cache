Derived comparison
------------------
Atomic group / plain-map control: 9.99x the CPU time
Atomic group / plain-map control: 1.00x measured allocations (both are zero)
Plain-map control / atomic group: 0.10x the CPU throughput

The plain-map control is intentionally weaker: it does not synchronize the
two maps, preflight all keys, or roll back an update. The 9.77x figure is the
cost of the correctness contract, not a claim that the group is a faster map.
The feature is opt-in and does not affect ordinary map or single-index paths.

Before allocation reuse optimization
-------------------------------------
The initial repeated-update implementation measured approximately 216.3
ns/op, 48 B/op, and 1 alloc/op for the atomic group. Reusing each existing
row's key slice reduced the update path to the measurements above: 208.7
ns/op median, 0 B/op, and 0 allocs/op.
